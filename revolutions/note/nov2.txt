	General information:
		Roger Williams in 1636 got banished from Massechuses Bay Colony for religous views. He got to Narragansett Bay
		and founded a city called Providence and made it a place of religous freedom.
		
		In 1638 a bunch of people talked to Roger Williams then started there own colony on the island rhode island Pocasett
		which was renamed Portsmouth in 1639. The town split into two with the south half becoming Newport. Portsmouth governing
		document was the Portsmouth compact.

		In 1642 Samuel Gorton bought land and made a town after becoming pissed with massechuses bc religon for his plantation.
		
		In 1644 Portsmouth, Providence, Newport, became Colony of Rhode Island and Providence Plantation. Samuel Gorten was president
		and the colony was run by him and elected council members.
		
		In 1686 King James II shoved Rhode island into the Dominon of New england, but they gained independence again in 1688
		after King James II got overthrown. 
	
		In 1688 they started owning slaves, but there was also no known evidence of laws allowing slave ownership. The colony
		got rich distiling rum to sell in Africa, prospered in triangle trade with slaves and sugar from Carribean.

		In 1652 owning slaves was made illegal, they were the first british colony to make this rule. This was not enforced	
		and they were still heavily involved in trading slaves.
	
		In 1774 slaves made up 6.3% of rhode islands total population.

		In 1772 Residents of Providence burned an english ship the HMS Gaspee because the british were enforcing trade
		regulations that they did not like.
	
		In 1776 they were the first of the 13 colonies to renounce there aligence to the british crown.

		Roger Williams was a Puritan Minister
	
		Very strong culture of Religous freedom and self governance

		Officialy became a confederancy under the Patent of 1643-1644 granted by the English Parliment. Then it was
		more well established by the Royal charter in 1663 by King Charles II, it granted Religous Liberties.

		In 1776 it became the State of Rhode Island and Providence Plantations after renouncing Britan.
		
		Became the fourth colony to ratify the Articles of Confederation in 1778

		1640-1774 pop grew from 300-59,607

		No official state church
